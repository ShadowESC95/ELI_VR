# ELI VR 0.9.14 — the rooms furnished, and the headset kept its room on the card

**The rooms are furnished with real things.** Thirty-nine more scanned models (CC0, Poly Haven, listed in assets/CREDITS.md) and every room that was still bare shapes has something in it to be among. The Core deck has a seating group behind you under a lamp hung from its rib, a bench at the rail facing Earth with binoculars on the rail, crates and a tool chest by the rail, planters and a camera on a rib. The Simulation Lab's towers have a shape (stepped blocks and masts) with the data arcing between them in pulses, and ELI's house stands as a hologram on a table: each room a tile of light, the ways between them threads, the room ELI is in burning brightest, this room marked. The Debug Basement has shelves of spare parts, a bench of instruments under the log board, pipes and a duct, alarms and caged lamps on the pillars, a generator, a dartboard and a sign for a floor that is never dry. The Deep Space platform has an instrument desk facing the giant, a bench, stores and a searchlight; the Arctic a fire with benches, a path of lamps to the hut, its woodpile and stores; the Ocean rig a lifebuoy and a light on its mast, lamps at the rail, a hose and containers; the Desert the astronomers' table under its red lamp; the Underwater lab its bench and shelves; the Forest real ferns, stumps, logs, mossy rocks and young pines close by, with lanterns on the posts.

**With ELI v3 3.0.151, the headset keeps its room on the card.** In 0.9.13 on 3.0.150 the first reply with the headset on failed inside CUDA and took ELI v3 with it: ELI had planned its model load before ELI VR and the VR runtime took the card. ELI v3 now measures what they hold on each card while you are in the rooms and keeps that much free at the next load, loads its model only once ELI VR is in the room when ELI started it, and runs one full batch through right after every load so its own buffers are at size before anyone else takes the card. The ELI'S MIND panel lists the models beside the one chosen in ELI v3's settings and in any folder named in ELI v3's `ELI_MODEL_DIRS`, not only ELI v3's own folder.

**Smaller:** a missing manifest of ELI's own fetched models, an empty socket packet or an empty reply body no longer print a JSON parse error.

Not yet: the scanned trees (hundreds of megabytes each) are not for a headset, so the forest's tall trunks stay drawn; pieces made part by part; the flat-window mouse on the desktop screen. The Simulation Lab builds headless and in the checks, but a flat-window picture of it would not render on the development machine's driver, with 0.9.13's room script as much as this one; the other fourteen rooms were pictured.
