# ELI VR 0.9.5 — a real library, and what ELI does for you in its house

The Memory Archive is a library now. Its walls are shelved floor to roof with books in leathers of every age, gilt on their spines, a few of them glowing and fading where ELI's memory is alive; library ladders run on brass rails, and warm lamps on the columns light the shelves where you walk to them. Three reading corners have armchairs, a table and an oil lamp that lights the books beside it; a librarian's desk, catalogue cabinets, a cabinet, a grandfather clock and a marble bust stand against the shelves. ELI's real memories stand face out on the shelves ahead of you at eye height, each a book with what ELI remembers written on its cover.

What ELI does for you now shows up in its house. What it found for you (a web search with its top results and their sites, the news, the weather) is printed and pinned on the board over the Evidence Wall's desk. What it wrote (a document, a file) is clipped to the rack in the Workshop with its opening lines. The radio on the low table in the Core Room plays what ELI is playing, its name and artist on a card above it, and says when you paused it.

Writing on a pinned sheet now starts inside the page; before, the beliefs on the Evidence Wall began at the middle of their sheet and ran off its edge.

New furniture is from Poly Haven (CC0, listed in `assets/CREDITS.md`).

What ELI found, wrote and is playing needs ELI v3 3.0.146 or newer; the library and the memories on its shelves work with any ELI v3 that ELI VR 0.9.4 works with.
