# ELI VR 0.9.1 — ELI is "it"

ELI is now called "it" everywhere: in the app's own words on the status line ("ELI answered, but its voice is not available"), in the room descriptions, and in every guide and note that comes with the download.

Nothing else has changed since [0.9.0](https://github.com/ShadowESC95/ELI_VR/releases/tag/v0.9.0); what it brought, and what has and has not been tried, is in its notes. The new parts with ELI v3 need ELI v3 3.0.134 or newer.
