# ELI VR 0.9.27 — fully offline, licence included

- **ELI VR never goes online for its licence.** 0.9.26 asked ELI's licence server, at each launch, for its list of withdrawn licences and its trial; that is gone, along with online activation and the network library that came with it. Nothing about the licence leaves this computer.
- **One key per computer.** A licence covers a number of computers. To use it on this one, send the seller your licence number and this computer's activation code (the control dock's **Licence…** shows it, with Copy), and enter the key you get back. **Deactivate this computer** removes the key here; tell the seller, who frees its seat for another computer.
- **Buy** points to geteli.tech, as every ELI product does.
- The seven-day trial from first use is unchanged, and kept on this computer.
