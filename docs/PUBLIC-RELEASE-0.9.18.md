# ELI VR 0.9.18 — real seabirds and a real black hole, books you can take down and read, an Evidence Wall of what ELI actually holds

**The Ocean.** The spinning prisms are gone: a flock of gulls with real wings beats and glides over the rig, banking into its turns and keeping to a gull's height over the water (the mountain gets buzzards circling a thermal). The sea is a real swell now, and the buoys, the boat and the foam ride the waves that are drawn instead of bobbing on their own. The boat is a proper clinker dinghy.

**The Desert** is a real night under the Milky Way: the stars dim and redden towards the horizon, the last twilight glows in the west, and the moon is back where its age puts it, waxing gibbous in the south-east, lighting the dunes from there at the brightness its phase gives.

**The Anomaly.** The ring and the glow are replaced by a black hole drawn from how light really bends round one: the shadow, the photon ring, a hot disc that is bluer and brighter on the side coming towards you, the far side of the disc arching over and under the shadow, and the stars behind bent into arcs and an Einstein ring. It is light enough for a headset GPU.

**Books you can take down and read.** Every book in the Memory Archive can be taken off its shelf with the grip, opened in the hand and read page by page (turn with the trigger, a flick of the stick, or the other hand across the page). Let go and it closes and goes back to its place. The Archive keeps them in time order with a plate for each month, and ELI's stories have shelves of their own. Text is sized for reading where you are, whatever your height.

**ELI's room** has a bookcase of ELI's own books: what it likes and dislikes, what it believes, its thoughts, its reflections on itself, its goals and its diary.

**The Evidence Wall** keeps its full wall of pinned sheets and threads, and every sheet now carries one of ELI's real records: what it holds about you, how sure it is and since when, disputes first, threads between related ones, a scoreboard of what its actions did, how often it was right week by week, what it learned and what its beliefs rest on. A spare place is blank paper, never invented text. It updates as ELI learns.

**Fixed:** letting go of a pinned record no longer sends ELI a "move" it rejects; a record with no place left no longer stands in another's slot.

Works with ELI v3 3.0.160 (books are read from it whole when they are long). Not yet checked in a headset: the reading size and the black hole's frame time were checked headless.
