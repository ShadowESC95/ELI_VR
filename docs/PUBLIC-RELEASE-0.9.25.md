# ELI VR 0.9.25 — the pages say what the app does

Nothing in the app changes. The README, settings, quality, network, commands, architecture, ELI and roadmap pages were checked against the code and corrected where they were wrong.

- The ELI link starts on `auto`, not off.
- Render scale, fog and the audio switch in the settings file are read by nothing yet, and the pages now say so instead of describing them as working.
- The architecture page names every script the app has now, and the ELI page lists every capability ELI VR asks ELI v3 for, including kept records: a record that has not changed is sent by its id alone and drawn from the copy already held.
- The Evidence Wall and the Anomaly Room are described as built, not as coming next.
- Frame-rate and memory figures that could not be measured from the repository were taken out.

The release build keeps its in-between files for three days.
