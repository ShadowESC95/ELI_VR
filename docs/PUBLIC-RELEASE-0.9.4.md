# ELI VR 0.9.4 — ELI's screens show what is really happening

The screens in ELI's house now show ELI's real state, in its own words and nothing made up. The Debug Basement's big board is ELI's log, newest at the bottom, with troubles in amber and what worked in green, and its four terminals each follow one strand of it: troubles, reasoning, recall and work. The Orbital Core's consoles show what ELI is doing and in what mood, its measures (focus, curiosity, uncertainty, evidence and memory confidence) as bars, its log, what it did lately and why, and what is on its mind. The Fabrication Lab shows ELI's goals and the improvements it has proposed or made; the Workshop shows what its tools did lately, what worked and what failed; the Evidence Wall shows its open questions, and ELI's beliefs are pinned to the wall as written sheets. A screen with nothing true to show says so, and when ELI is not connected every screen says that and nothing else.

ELI VR started on its own now finds ELI v3 on the port ELI v3 says it serves on. With ELI v2 running on port 8081, ELI VR used to find ELI v2, which has no rooms, and ELI stayed dormant.

The screens need ELI v3 3.0.144 or newer. Finding ELI v3 on a moved port needs ELI v3 3.0.145 or newer.
