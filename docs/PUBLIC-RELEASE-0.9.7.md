# ELI VR 0.9.7 — what ELI saw, what is coming up, and ELI acting on its own

The pictures ELI took or looked at for you (a screenshot, an image it read) are pinned up as photographs on a board beside the Evidence Wall's desk, each with what ELI saw in it. ELI VR fetches each picture from ELI v3 once; ELI v3 serves only a picture its own evidence ledger records as one ELI took or looked at for you.

A screen on the Core Room's deck, opposite the radio, shows what is coming up on your agenda in the next day and a half: events, reminders and alarms, each with when.

ELI acts of its own accord more often and with more reason: what it just did for you (a search, a song, a document, a picture) and the turn of the day are now moments it may make something of in its house, or leave be.

Needs ELI v3 3.0.148 or newer for the pictures, the agenda and the new moments.
