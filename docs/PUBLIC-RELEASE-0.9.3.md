# ELI VR 0.9.3 — ELI lives in its rooms

ELI is no longer a light pinned to one spot. It moves among what is its in the room (what it made, its memories, goals and beliefs) and stops to look at each. It comes to you while you talk to it, while it thinks about what you said and while it answers. When it makes or changes something it goes there and pours its light into it while it grows; when it relights a room it rises to the middle while the light changes. When ELI's world takes it to another room you see it rise and fade, with a word on where it went, and it flies back in when it returns. Now and then a line of what is on its mind rises from it and fades: its latest insight, a moment of its own waiting to be made into something, the goal it holds first, its tone. And when such a moment is waiting and ELI is free, it makes something of it while you are there to see, in whichever room of its house it chooses: never while you talk to it, one at a time, paced by how long the last one took.

ELI's light is far more detailed: a living core, a skin of shifting colour and motes around it, and its colour is how it feels, each expression its own, moving smoothly from one to the next. The feeling shows on ELI itself; the room is not washed in it.

ELI can relight any room of the house. The colour it chooses becomes that room's own light, worn by its glowing trims, strips, screens and points of light; the sky, the ground and the air stay the room's own. The Evidence Wall now holds what ELI believes about you, each with when it learned it (amber where something disputes it), and the Anomaly Room each thing ELI was told two ways, with the question that would settle it.

The rooms that looked empty are furnished with real objects from Poly Haven (CC0, listed in `assets/CREDITS.md`): desks with old monitors, meters and boards in the Debug Basement; a desk under its lamp, a projector, a ceiling fan and a cabinet of files in the Evidence Wall; vices, toolboxes, shelves, a tool chest and a drill press in the Workshop; a test bench with its microscope in the Upgrade Bay. Their dark halves are lit.

ELI VR puts itself on ELI v3's list of bundles every time it starts, and the link to ELI is on by itself wherever ELI v3 is installed.

The room changes and ELI moving to make things need ELI v3 3.0.143 or newer. ELI's thoughts, its acting while you are there, and the Evidence Wall and Anomaly Room records need ELI v3 3.0.144 or newer.
