# ELI VR 0.9.6 — ELI furnishes and paints its rooms

ELI can now decorate with real things. When ELI VR links to ELI v3 it tells ELI every real object it can draw (the chairs, tables, lamps, cabinets, books, clocks and the rest in `assets/models/`), what each is and how big it is. ELI can place any of them in any room of its house, at its real size or scaled, set one on another (a lamp on a table), and turn it the way it should face; a thing nobody turned faces the middle of the room.

ELI can also paint. A painting is ELI's own: a style (rings, stripes, waves, cells, blooms, horizon, rays or drift) in two to four colours of its choosing, every one different. It hangs where ELI puts it, and one set on the floor stands on an easel.

The Upgrade Bay has panelled walls and a bank of glass-fronted racks across its far wall, their status lights blinking as hard as ELI is working.

Real things and paintings need ELI v3 3.0.147 or newer.
